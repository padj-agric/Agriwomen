About Agriwomen

Agriwomen is a platform dedicated to creating opportunities for young girls and women through agriculture.

We believe that when women have access to knowledge, skills, resources and economic opportunities, they can transform their families, communities and the future.

Our Objectives

Building stronger women and stronger communities.

👩🏾‍🌾

Women Empowerment

Empowering young girls and women with agricultural knowledge, skills and opportunities.

💼

Entrepreneurship

Supporting women to create businesses, generate income and become economically independent.

🥗

Food Security

Improving access to healthy, sustainable and locally grown food for families.

🌍

Climate Adaptation

Teaching eco-friendly farming practices to adapt to environmental changes.

🛡️

Fight Against Violence

Creating safe spaces and economic independence to protect women from gender-based violence.
